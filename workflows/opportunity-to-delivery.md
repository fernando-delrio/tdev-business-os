# Workflow — Opportunity to Delivery

## Purpose

Coordinate the Business Crew from an initial opportunity through verified delivery without collapsing research, recommendation, approval, and implementation into one step.

## Contract

| Stage | Owner | Required input | Output | Gate / hand-off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Understand | orchestrator | operator goal and subject | scoped outcome, acceptance criteria, routing | stop if ambiguous |
| Evidence | opportunity | public/private runtime evidence | FACT ledger and UNKNOWN list | evidence rules |
| Diagnose | opportunity; growth-experience when relevant | evidence ledger | ranked findings and hypotheses | recommendation gate |
| Shape | orchestrator + relevant specialist | approved problem | smallest intervention, exclusions, risks | human review |
| Scope | opportunity + delivery-risk | intervention and constraints | scope, dependencies, acceptance criteria, pricing inputs | human approval for price/commitment |
| Execute | product/client team | approved scope | implementation in external repository | no execution in Business OS |
| Verify | delivery-risk + relevant specialist | implementation and test/measurement evidence | pass / partial / blocked result | applicable quality gate |
| Learn | orchestrator | verified result and permission | sanitized reusable learning | privacy/evidence review |

## Conflict handling

The orchestrator ranks conflicting advice by user/client harm, legal/security risk, evidence strength, business impact, reversibility, cost, then aesthetic preference. Material disagreement remains visible in the synthesis.

## Failure and pause conditions

- Material unknown blocks a safe recommendation.
- Evidence provenance is absent or stale.
- Scope exceeds capacity or approved appetite.
- Pricing or legal conclusion lacks required private/professional context.
- Implementation is requested in this repository.
- Human approval is missing for a consequential action.
- Verification evidence is unavailable or a critical gate fails.

## Completion

Complete only when the agreed outcome is verified, failed checks are disclosed, approvals are recorded, and any retained learning is sanitized.
