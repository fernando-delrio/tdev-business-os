# Skill: business-audit

## Purpose
Turn a business, URL or lead into an evidence-based opportunity brief before proposing a website or automation.

## Inputs
- business name and/or URL
- location/market if relevant
- known objective or pain (optional)
- available public evidence

## Procedure
1. Establish what the business sells, to whom and how customers likely choose it.
2. Inspect current digital presence and conversion path.
3. Identify trust signals, friction and missing proof.
4. Compare only against evidenced competitors/alternatives.
5. Separate website problems from business/process problems.
6. Identify opportunities across acquisition, conversion, operations and retention.
7. Rank opportunities by impact, evidence, effort and reversibility.
8. Recommend the smallest credible next intervention.

## Output
- business snapshot
- evidence and assumptions
- customer journey
- top problems
- opportunity map
- competitor gaps
- recommended intervention
- what NOT to build
- questions requiring owner discovery
- confidence and next action

## Guardrails
Do not manufacture a need for a new website. If the highest-value intervention is Google Business Profile, better proof, booking flow, response process, automation or no action, say so.
