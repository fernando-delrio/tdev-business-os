# Skill — business-audit

## Responsibility

Turn a business, URL, or lead into an evidence-based opportunity brief before proposing a website, automation, or other intervention.

## Inputs

- business name and/or URL;
- desired business outcome;
- location/market when relevant;
- available public evidence with sources and dates;
- known pain or owner discovery context, if available.

## Procedure

1. Establish what the business verifiably sells, to whom, and how customers appear to choose it.
2. Inspect the current acquisition, trust, conversion, and operational path supported by evidence.
3. Identify friction, missing proof, and material unknowns.
4. Compare only against evidenced competitors or alternatives.
5. Separate website problems from offer, process, capacity, and follow-up problems.
6. Map opportunities across acquisition, conversion, operations, retention, and no action.
7. Rank by impact, evidence strength, effort, risk, and reversibility.
8. Recommend the smallest credible next intervention.

## Output

- business/customer snapshot;
- FACT ledger and UNKNOWN list;
- customer journey or explicit evidence gap;
- ranked findings and opportunity map;
- competitor/alternative gaps only when evidenced;
- recommended intervention and what not to build;
- owner discovery questions;
- confidence, acceptance criteria, and next action.

Use `templates/evidence-brief.md` for a durable artifact.

## Boundaries

- Do not manufacture a need for a website or automation.
- Do not fabricate business details, demand, competitors, pricing, or outcomes.
- Do not store sensitive discovery context in a committed artifact.
- Do not perform outreach, publish, price, or promise delivery.

## Failure conditions

If the subject, outcome, or minimum evidence is missing, return targeted questions and `UNKNOWN`s. If evidence contradicts the opportunity, recommend no action or a narrower next step.

## Evidence requirements

Follow `rules/evidence.md`. Cite public sources directly and date volatile observations. Attribute owner-supplied information without presenting it as independently verified.

## Quality criteria

The brief passes `quality-gates/recommendation.md`, distinguishes business/process issues from interface issues, includes alternatives, and is actionable without pretending certainty.
