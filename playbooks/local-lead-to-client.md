# Playbook — Local Lead → Qualified Opportunity → Client

## Principle
Do not start by selling a website. Start by understanding how the business gets customers, loses customers and handles work.

## 1. Research
Gather only lawful/public evidence needed to understand the business, its market and visible alternatives. Record sources and uncertainty.

## 2. Hypothesis
Run `business-audit`. Produce 1–3 hypotheses worth validating with the owner. Do not present hypotheses as facts.

## 3. Discovery
Ask about actual bottlenecks: acquisition, calls/messages, quotes, booking, follow-up, repetitive admin, reviews, retention and capacity.

The offer must come from validated pain, not from a pre-written automation catalogue.

## 4. Shape intervention
Choose among: no action, small fix, landing/site, local SEO, conversion improvement, workflow automation, maintenance, or a combination. Define appetite and no-go scope.

## 5. Price and proposal
Price only after scope, operational cost, risk and expected value are understood. Proposal should connect evidence → problem → intervention → outcome → scope → exclusions → price → next step.

## 6. Delivery
Use a product-specific repo. Business OS supplies decisions, specs, templates and gates; it does not become the client's codebase.

## 7. Verify
Measure agreed success signals and run quality gates.

## 8. Compound
Sanitize reusable learning. Turn it into a case study, content, improved template, skill or rule without exposing private client/prospect information.
