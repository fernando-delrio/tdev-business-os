# Playbook — Local Lead → Qualified Opportunity → Client

## Outcome

Decide whether and how to pursue a local-business opportunity without manufacturing a need for a website or automation.

## Inputs

- business identity and lawful public sources;
- location/market and known objective;
- owner-approved discovery context when available;
- current operator capacity and private commercial constraints.

## Process

### 1. Research

The `opportunity` agent gathers only the public evidence required to understand the business, customer choice, visible alternatives, and acquisition path. Record sources, dates, and unknowns.

### 2. Hypothesis

Run `business-audit`. Produce one to three hypotheses worth validating with the owner. Each is an `INFERENCE`, never a fact.

### 3. Discovery

Ask about acquisition, calls/messages, quotes, booking, follow-up, repetitive administration, reviews, retention, capacity, and desired outcomes. Record consented context privately when it contains sensitive information.

### 4. Shape the intervention

Choose the smallest credible option: no action, small fix, landing/site, local SEO, conversion improvement, workflow automation, maintenance, or a justified combination. Define appetite, dependencies, acceptance criteria, and no-go scope.

### 5. Scope and price

Use current private inputs for cost, capacity, risk, margin, and expected value. Connect evidence → problem → intervention → outcome → scope → exclusions → price → next step. Human approval is required before a commitment or client-facing proposal.

### 6. Delivery

Implement in the product/client repository. This repository supplies decisions, specifications, templates, and gates—not client source code.

### 7. Verify

Run the relevant quality gates against production-like evidence. Failed or unmeasured checks remain visible.

### 8. Compound

With permission, sanitize reusable learning into intelligence, a template, a skill improvement, or a case study. Never expose prospect/client information or invent outcomes.

## Outputs

- qualified / nurture / decline decision;
- evidence brief and open discovery questions;
- approved intervention, scope boundaries, and success signals;
- delivery gate result;
- optional sanitized learning record.

## Stop conditions

Stop when there is no credible problem, discovery contradicts the hypothesis, capacity makes delivery unsafe, evidence is insufficient for pricing, or approval is missing at a consequential boundary.

## Quality criteria

The process preserves provenance, distinguishes hypotheses from facts, includes a no-action option, minimizes scope, protects private data, and records who approved the commercial commitment.
